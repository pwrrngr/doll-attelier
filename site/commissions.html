Private commissions

A garment madefor her alone.

Every commission is a conversation before it is a garment. A limited number are accepted each season, from collectors anywhere in the world.

  1. i.

    Enquiry

    Send photographs of your doll and a few words about the garment you imagine.

  2. ii.

    Consultation

    We discuss her era, condition and character, and agree measurements.

  3. iii.

    Proposal

    You receive a design study, material suggestions, a timeline and a quotation.

  4. iv.

    The making

    Pattern, toile and handwork — with progress shared at key stages.

  5. v.

    Delivery

    Documented, wrapped in archival tissue and sent to you, wherever you are.

Questions

Before you begin.

Which dolls do you dress?

Antique and antique-style dolls of many makers and sizes. If you are unsure whether your doll is suitable, send a photograph and her measurements and we will advise.

Can you recreate a garment from an old photograph?

Yes — period photographs, fashion plates and family pictures are among the best starting points. The result is an original garment sympathetic to the reference, not a copy presented as antique.

How long does a commission take?

It depends on the garment and the season's calendar. A timeline is agreed with you at the proposal stage, before any work begins.

Do you send garments abroad?

Yes. Garments have been sent to collectors in Japan, England and Spain. Shipping is arranged for each commission and confirmed in your proposal.

Is the garment documented?

Every piece leaves with an atelier record: photographs, materials and design notes, so its history travels with the doll.
