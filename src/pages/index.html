Heritage l’héritage

A European art of dress.

The great antique dolls were made in the porcelain and bisque workshops of France and Germany, and dressed in the fashions of Paris and Vienna. To dress one well is to speak the language of European couture — its silhouettes, its cloth, its hand finishing.

It is the language {{MAKER}} has worked in for forty years, designing and making dresses before her work found its most intimate scale.
