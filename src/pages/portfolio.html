The privatewardrobe.

Not a catalogue — a record of singular pieces. Each garment was made once, for one doll, and now lives in a private collection.

i.

The Pearl Parlour Gown

Grand toilette · after 1890

An ivory silk gown for afternoon receiving, its bodice set with seed pearls and its skirt swept back into a long train of rose tulle. Paired with a matching silk hat trimmed in ostrich and silk flowers.

Silhouette
Fitted bodice, draped bustle, train
Materials
Ivory silk, rose tulle, seed pearls, antique lace
Handwork
Pearl setting, gathered tulle, lace edging
Collection
Private collection
ii.

The Blue Hour

Promenade · after 1904

A walking dress in the soft blue of early evening. The bodice is pintucked and closed with a row of covered buttons; a knife-pleated panel opens the front of the skirt, and the hem is banded with fine tucks.

Silhouette
High collar, fitted waist, A-line skirt
Materials
Silk faille, cotton lawn, antique lace
Handwork
Pintucks, covered buttons, knife pleats
Collection
Private collection
iii.

A Study in Oxblood

Evening · after 1888

Deep oxblood silk velvet worked with scrolling soutache and strung jet, the skirt falling in swagged tiers. Finished with a jet choker and earrings, in the manner of late-Victorian evening dress.

Silhouette
Pointed bodice, tiered swag skirt
Materials
Silk velvet, soutache braid, jet beads, lace
Handwork
Braid couching, bead embroidery
Collection
Private collection
