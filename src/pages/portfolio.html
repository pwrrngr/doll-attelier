The privatewardrobe.

A record of singular pieces. Each garment was made once, for one doll, and drafted, cut and finished by hand in the atelier.

i.

The Rose Garland Ensemble

Coat, dress & bonnet · in the manner of the 1880s

A long coat cut from ivory silk woven with stripes and trailing roses, the pattern matched across every seam and laid into deep pleats at the back. Beneath it, a pink silk dress with covered buttons and a jabot of antique lace; above, a ruched silk bonnet with flowers.

Ensemble
Coat, dress, bonnet
Materials
Striped silk with rose garlands, pink silk, antique lace
Handwork
Pattern matching, box pleats, covered buttons, lace cuffs
Inside
Fully lined in silk; waist tape and pleated underlayers
ii.

Sage & Lace, after the Catalogue

Dress & accessories · after a period engraving

Drawn from an engraving in a period doll catalogue: costume extra riche, satin et dentelle. Ivory silk worked with lace, a front laced in sage satin, and tiered pleated flounces, with a matching parasol, plumed hat and silk shoes.

Ensemble
Dress, hat, parasol, shoes
Materials
Ivory silk, embroidered lace, sage satin ribbon
Handwork
Lacing, pleated flounces, ribbon sash
Reference
Period catalogue engraving
iii.

Grey Silk with Raised Lace

Dress & hat · in the manner of the 1890s

A striped grey silk with a scattered diamond pattern, cut with the full sleeves of the 1890s. The bodice carries a bib of raised antique lace over pintucked tulle; a pink silk sash and a hat in the same silk, trimmed with roses and tulle, complete it.

Ensemble
Dress, hat
Materials
Patterned striped silk, raised antique lace, pink silk
Handwork
Full sleeves, pintucked front, lace appliqué
iv.

Ivory Lace & Parasol

Dress, hat & parasol · in the manner of the 1880s

Embroidered tulle lace layered over ivory silk, with a pointed bodice, short puffed sleeves and a pleated silk hem. A straw hat and a small silk parasol finish the summer ensemble.

Ensemble
Dress, hat, parasol
Materials
Embroidered tulle lace, ivory silk, straw
Handwork
Layered lace, pleated hem, sleeve bows
v.

Aqua Silk & Lace Insertion

Dress & bonnet · in the manner of c. 1900

Pale aqua silk with a deep collar of antique lace edged in tiny pearls, bands of lace insertion at the bodice, cuffs and skirt, and appliqué lace motifs. A soft bonnet of the same lace accompanies it.

Ensemble
Dress, bonnet
Materials
Aqua silk, antique lace, pearl trim
Handwork
Lace insertion, appliqué, gathered tiers
vi.

Cream Wool & Soutache

Dress · in the manner of the 1880s

Fine cream wool worked with scrolling soutache braid down the front and around the hem and cuffs, opening over a draped and pleated silk front. Layers of pleated silk and antique lace show at the hem.

Ensemble
Dress
Materials
Cream wool, soutache braid, patterned silk, antique lace
Handwork
Soutache couching, knife pleats, lace ruffles
